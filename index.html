Welcome

This watchlist reads market data straight from free data providers. Each provider needs its own free API key, which you paste in once. Keys are kept only in this browser on this phone.

  1. Finnhub (required): prices, company info, metrics, analyst ratings and earnings dates. Free key at finnhub.io/register.
  2. Twelve Data (optional): price charts, sparklines and a live USD/AUD rate. Free key at twelvedata.com/register.
  3. Marketstack (optional): ASX end-of-day closes in AUD, 100 requests a month. Free key at marketstack.com. You can add it later in Settings.

            How alerts work

            Alerts are checked on this device only while the app is open: when it opens and on every refresh. A price alert fires once when the price crosses your level and re-arms after it moves back. A daily-move alert fires at most once per trading day. Background push (alerts while the app is closed) needs a server, which this static app doesn't have, so it isn't supported.

            API keys

            Saved only in this browser's localStorage on this device and sent only to the provider it belongs to. They are never written to any file.

            About the data

            Finnhub free (60 calls/min): quote, company profile, basic financials, recommendation trends, earnings calendar. Price targets and candles are premium, so price targets show n/a (not on free tier).

            Twelve Data free (8 credits/min, 800/day): price history for the chart and the 1-month sparklines (1M, 6M and 1Y share one daily series), and the live USD/AUD rate. ASX symbols are not on the free plan.

            Marketstack free (100 requests/month, every symbol counts): the only free source we found that a web page can call for ASX prices. End-of-day closes only, about 1 year of history. Nothing ASX loads on a timer except your portfolio holdings.

            Frankfurter (no key): ECB daily reference USD/AUD rate, used when there's no Twelve Data key.

            Not available free for ASX: live or intraday prices, analyst ratings, price targets, metrics, news and earnings dates. These show n/a with the reason.

            Responses are cached on this device: quotes 1 min, news 30 min, metrics and ratings 12 h, earnings calendar 6 h, profiles 7 days, charts 5 min (1D) to 24 h (5Y), ASX closes until the next close is out (6 pm Sydney time, trading days), USD/AUD 10 min (live) or 6 h (ECB). Data may be delayed. Not financial advice.
